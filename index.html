Welcome to VINYYLE

Guess the mystery album in six tries. Search by album title or artist. Each guess reveals clues.

  • Green: exact match for that category.
  • Yellow for year means within 5 years (but not exact). Yellow for genre means a different genre within the same broad family (Rock, Hip-Hop or Pop). Country, artist, group/individual and decade never turn yellow.
  • Red: no exact or qualifying close match.
  • ↑ means the mystery album is newer than your guess; ↓ means it is older. Group / Individual indicates whether the credited artist is a group or an individual. Decade only turns green on an exact decade match.

Easy mode selects from a smaller set of iconic albums; Hard mode uses the full starter catalog. Each mode starts with a daily puzzle. After finishing, Play again starts a new puzzle in that mode.
